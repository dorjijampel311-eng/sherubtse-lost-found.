About this project

A more helpful campus lost & found board.

This student project gives Sherubtse College students and staff one place to share lost and found item reports and contact the person who posted them.

Use the board thoughtfully

  • Post only information that helps identify the item.
  • Meet in a public campus place when arranging a handover.
  • Never post passwords, bank details, or full ID numbers.
  • Use “My posts” to mark an item returned or delete a report.

This project is a community noticeboard. Listings are provided by users and are not independently verified by the college.
